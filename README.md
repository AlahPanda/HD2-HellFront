# HD2 — HellFront

**A curated Helldivers 2 modpack focused on immersive warfare, enhanced audio, custom models, and cinematic combat.**

HellFront is a collection of mods I personally use to make Helldivers 2 feel more intense, immersive, and cinematic.

I've spent quite some time experimenting with different mods, adjusting their settings, and finding combinations that work well together. Eventually, I decided to share my setup so other players could try it without having to configure everything from scratch.

The idea is simple: make the battlefield look and sound more like an actual war, while keeping the original gameplay intact.

HellFront is distributed as an Arsenal Mod Manager profile. All third-party mods belong to their respective creators, and their original pages are linked below.

## What Does HellFront Change?

Quite a lot, actually lol.

Most of the changes focus on the atmosphere of combat rather than how the game plays.

### Voices & Communications

- Replaces Pelican-1 and Eagle-1 pilot communications with military-inspired voice lines.
- Adds Insurgency Sandstorm voice packs for different combat voices.
- Includes Halo-inspired BUCK voice lines.
- Changes selected enemy and character voices.
- Adds Titanfall-inspired Exosuit radio chatter.
- Changes certain Democracy Officer dialogue and character exertion sounds.

### Weapons & Combat Audio

- Reworks ballistic weapon sounds to make gunfire feel heavier.
- Enhances audio for both heavy and smaller enemy units.
- Changes selected sentry turret sounds.
- Improves Eagle-1 airstrike and 500kg bomb audio.
- Adds custom explosion effects and an ICBM alarm.
- Enhances general battlefield ambience.

### Weapons & Equipment

- Replaces selected kinetic, laser, and plasma weapon models.
- Adds alternative weapon attachments and visual details.
- Changes the appearance of certain sentry turrets.
- Introduces military-inspired equipment and cosmetic replacements.

### Vehicles & Exosuits

- Changes the appearance of the FRV and Supply FRV.
- Adds alternative military-style vehicle models.
- Replaces selected Exosuit appearances.
- Includes Pelican-1 Reimagined, based on the SF Dropship R35.
- Includes a UNSC Sabre-inspired replacement for Eagle-1.

### Super Destroyer & Environment

- Adds UNSC-inspired fleet visuals.
- Changes selected SEAF personnel and crew appearances.
- Replaces certain NPC models and Democracy Officer equipment.
- Improves emissive materials, lighting details, and stratagem beacon effects.

### Music & Extraction

- Includes optional orchestral music modifications.
- Supports my custom PTSD Without Reason extraction soundtrack as an additional mod.
- Offers an optional Earthmover scream during extraction.
- Adds a custom soundtrack ending during Pelican-1 departure.

**Important:** Some music mods modify the same extraction audio resources and may conflict. Check the music compatibility notes before combining them.

Not every change will appear in every mission. Some depend on the weapons, vehicles, enemies, stratagems, and equipment being used.

HellFront also uses my personally selected variants and configurations, so your setup should reflect the choices I made where the profile and available mod files support them.

## Installation

HellFront is designed to be installed through **Arsenal Mod Manager**.

1. Install Arsenal Mod Manager.
2. [Download the latest HellFront profile](https://github.com/AlahPanda/HD2-HellFront/releases/latest).
3. Import the `.hd2a_profile` file into Arsenal.
4. Download the required mods through Arsenal's Nexus Mods integration.
5. Install the additional mods listed below if you want the complete setup.
6. Verify the selected options, dependencies, and load order.
7. Apply the profile when everything is correctly configured.

I recommend backing up your current Arsenal profile before importing HellFront.

Some mods may not be downloaded automatically, particularly those originally installed outside Arsenal's Nexus integration.

If anything is missing, use the original download links provided below.

### Music Compatibility

My mod [**PTSD Without Reason (Ultrakill)**](https://www.nexusmods.com/helldivers2/mods/18711) replaces the extraction soundtrack.

It may conflict with other music replacements, including configurations of Epic Orchestral Music Overhaul that modify the same extraction audio.

I recommend using a configuration that leaves the extraction music untouched when combining both mods.

You can also create a personal compatibility patch or modify PTSD Without Reason for your own use. Tools such as Codex can help with this, provided you understand which audio resources are being modified.

Personal modifications are allowed, but redistributing modified versions of my mod requires my permission. Please respect the permissions of other creators and music artists as well.
